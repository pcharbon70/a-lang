# Archive (`90-archive`)

## Purpose

The archive retains inactive, superseded, or historically useful material
without leaving it in active paths. Archiving preserves context and links; it
is not a substitute for deletion when material has no continuing value.

## What belongs here

- Superseded documents whose history still matters.
- Paused bodies of work that are no longer active.
- Legacy structures retained to avoid breaking important references.

When moving a document here, say why it was archived and link to its
replacement when one exists.
