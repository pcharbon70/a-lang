# Journal (`50-journal`)

## Purpose

The journal holds dated observations and exploratory writing. It preserves the
path of thought—including uncertainty and changes of mind—without requiring
every entry to become a polished note.

## What belongs here

- Dated research logs and session notes.
- Reflections on what changed or became clearer.
- Exploratory connections that are not ready for durable notes.

Name entries `YYYY-MM-DD.md`. If more than one entry is needed on a date, add a
short descriptive suffix.
