# Maps (`10-maps`)

## Purpose

Maps are curated paths through the archive. They connect notes, sources, and
inquiries into a useful line of thought without forcing the material into a
rigid subject hierarchy.

## What belongs here

- The archive's main entry point.
- Topic maps that introduce a field or cluster of documents.
- Question maps that organize competing answers or research trails.

A map should be selective and explanatory. It should tell a reader why its
links belong together, not merely repeat a file listing.
