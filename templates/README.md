# Templates (`templates`)

## Purpose

Templates provide consistent starting points for the archive's document kinds.
They encode the required frontmatter fields and offer a light structure for
the Markdown body.

Copy a template into the appropriate directory, rename it, and replace every
braced placeholder. Templates are examples and are not themselves archive
documents to validate as completed notes.

## Index

### Subdirectories

- None yet.

### Templates

- [Inquiry template](inquiry.md) — an active question, hypotheses, evidence,
  and next actions.
- [Journal template](journal.md) — a dated research or reflection entry.
- [Map template](map.md) — a curated route through related archive material.
- [Note template](note.md) — an idea or synthesis in the author's own words.
- [Source template](source.md) — a bibliographic record and evidence-focused
  reading note.

## Maintaining this index

Update this page whenever a template is added, renamed, or its intended use
changes. Keep every template aligned with
[`frontmatter.schema.json`](../frontmatter.schema.json).
