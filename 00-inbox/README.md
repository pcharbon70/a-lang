# Inbox (`00-inbox`)

## Purpose

This is the archive's low-friction capture point. Put material here when it is
worth retaining but its durable form, subject, or relevance is not yet clear.
The inbox is a temporary workspace rather than a permanent category.

## What belongs here

- Fleeting ideas, questions, and observations.
- Unprocessed links, quotations, and reading leads.
- Rough fragments awaiting review.

During review, turn worthwhile captures into a note, source, inquiry, map, or
journal entry. Delete or archive anything that no longer merits attention.
