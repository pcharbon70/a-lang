# Assets (`assets`)

## Purpose

Assets contains non-Markdown material used by archive documents: images,
diagrams, PDFs, datasets, and other attachments that should travel with the
archive.

## What belongs here

- Locally retained figures, illustrations, and diagrams.
- PDFs or datasets that may be stored and redistributed lawfully.
- Supporting files referenced by notes, sources, inquiries, or maps.

Prefer stable, descriptive filenames. Record provenance and licensing beside
an asset or in the document that introduces it; do not copy material into the
archive when a link is sufficient.

## Maintaining this index

Index assets by their role and link to the documents that use them. When a
group becomes large, place it in a descriptive subdirectory with its own
README.
