# Notes (`20-notes`)

## Purpose

Notes develop ideas in the author's own words. They are where observations,
comparisons, models, arguments, and syntheses become durable parts of the
archive.

## What belongs here

- Atomic ideas that can be understood and linked independently.
- Developing arguments and conceptual models.
- Syntheses that connect multiple sources or research threads.

Use `maturity` in frontmatter to distinguish a `seed`, `developing`, or
`stable` note. Reading records and bibliographic details belong in
[`30-sources`](../30-sources/README.md).
