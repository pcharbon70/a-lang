# Inquiries (`40-inquiries`)

## Purpose

Inquiries are active research workbenches. They hold questions that need
investigation, competing hypotheses, evidence requirements, and the next
useful steps.

## What belongs here

- Open research questions with enough structure to guide work.
- Paused inquiries worth preserving for later.
- Resolved inquiries whose reasoning remains useful.

Use the `status` frontmatter field to mark an inquiry as `open`, `paused`, or
`resolved`. Move reusable conclusions into notes rather than treating the
inquiry as the final synthesis.
